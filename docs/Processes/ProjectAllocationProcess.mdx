# Project allocation process

<ControlledDocBanner />

## Rule

Whenever an OETer is to be allocated to a new project, or their allocation to an existing project changes significantly, the allocation must be agreed by three parties:

1. The **Head of Department** (HoD) of the OETer.
2. The **Project Lead** of each current project whose allocation of the OETer changes as a result.
3. The **Project Lead** of the future project.

Project Leads of current projects whose allocation stays unchanged do not need to be involved.

The allocation is only officially planned once all three parties have agreed. It does not matter from whom the initiative comes: the OETer, an HoD, or a Project Lead. Whoever initiates the allocation is responsible for bringing all three parties into the conversation.

## Why

Allocations affect capacity across projects. A new allocation often reduces the time available for current projects. Involving all three parties early avoids surprises, protects delivery of running projects, and keeps the HoD's overview of the department's workload accurate.

## Process

All communication about the allocation happens by email, in a single thread. This keeps the agreement traceable and easy to find later.

1. **Initiate:** The initiator writes one email to the HoD and all affected Project Leads, with the OETer in CC. State the project, the expected share of time, and the start and end dates.
2. **Agree:** The parties discuss the impact on current projects in the same thread, and agree on the allocation or on an adjusted version of it. Each party confirms explicitly by replying to the thread. If the parties cannot agree, the HoD escalates to the Heads of Department meeting.
3. **Document:** Once all parties have confirmed, the Project Lead of the future project records the allocation in the [project planning sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1nsHaoue3-Hpu9hfgIc5ZIdQ5xexGQsYk/edit?usp=sharing&ouid=109094839317373979866&rtpof=true&sd=true) and replies to the thread to close it, adding the Finance department in CC so they can confirm the feasibility of the staffing plan.

If no current project allocation changes, the agreement between the HoD and the Project Lead of the future project is sufficient.
