# Travel Policy

<ControlledDocBanner />

## **1. Purpose**
This policy pertains to travel with or on behalf of OET. It applies to our entire company, across all teams and regions, and relates to any kind of travel, e.g., for conferences, workshops, or social events.

## **2. Updates**
- The policy will be reviewed annually, and may be updated
    - If an update is made, a new version will be published here, replacing the previous one

## **4. Questions**
