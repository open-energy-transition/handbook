# Identity & Access Management Policy
OET-ISMS-POL-003

## Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to define required access control measures to all Open Energy Transition (OET) (hereinafter referred to as the Organization) systems and applications to protect the privacy, security, and confidentiality of information technology resources.

## Scope

This policy applies to those responsible for the management of user accounts or access to shared information or network devices. Such information can be held within a database, application or shared file space. This policy covers departmental accounts as well as those managed centrally.

# Third-Party Management Procedure
OET-ISMS-POL-011

## Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the rules for secure (from an information security perspective) third-party management within OET.

Additionally, the document defines the elements that must be included in contracts with suppliers when, during the execution of the contract, there are obligations related to:
- ensuring information security,
- personal data processing, and
- maintaining the continuity of OET's delivery service.

## Scope

This procedure covers the entire scope of OET’s information security management system, including all OET information systems and other information resources.

# Risk Management Handbook
OET-ISMS-POL-017

## Purpose and Objective

The following Risk Management Handbook outlines the systematic identification, assessment and treatment of risks within Open Energy Transition (“OET”). It aims to provide a clear and structured approach to identifying risks early and taking actions to minimize negative impacts while optimizing opportunities. The objective is to ensure that risk management is conducted consistently and efficiently across the organization, fostering a shared understanding of the risk management process among all relevant stakeholders.

## Scope of Application
This handbook applies comprehensively across all departments and levels within OET. It is designed to support the Information Security Officer (”ISO”) in the centralized coordination and monitoring of the entire risk management process. Additionally, this handbook serves as a guide for all risk owners who play a role in identifying and assessing risks within their specific domains. By following the procedures outlined, each responsible party contributes to a cohesive and proactive risk management strategy that aligns with the company’s overall objectives.

# Business Continuity Management
OET-ISMS-POL-018

## Purpose

The purpose of this Business Continuity Management (BCM) policy is to establish a framework for ensuring the resilience of OET, enabling it to respond effectively to incidents that could disrupt business operations. 

## Scope

This document covers the entire scope of the Information Security Management System, including all information systems and other information resources. The users of this document are all employees, including contractors, of the OET. This Business Continuity Plan applies to all business processes, personnel, third parties, and information systems supporting critical organizational activities.

# Management Review Policy
OET-ISMS-POL-019

## Purpose

The purpose of this procedure is to ensure that top management periodically (once in a year) reviews the Information Security Management System (ISMS) to evaluate its performance, effectiveness, and alignment with organizational objectives and regulatory requirements.

## Scope

This procedure applies to the entire ISMS and all organizational units included within the ISMS scope. It must be performed at least once per year, or whenever significant changes occur.

# Background Check Policy
OET-ISMS-POL-023

## Purpose

This policy defines OET’s approach to conducting background checks on prospective employees and contractors. It ensures that hiring decisions are informed, proportionate to risk, and compliant with applicable laws, while safeguarding candidate privacy and data security.

## Scope

This policy applies globally to:
- ll candidates considered for employment or long-term engagement with OET
- Employment arrangements including:
- Direct hires
- Employees engaged via Employer of Record (EoR) providers (e.g., Remote.com)
- Contractors and advisors, where relevant

# Vulnerability Management Policy
OET-ISMS-POL-025

## Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to minimize the risk of unauthorized access or service disruption by identifying, evaluating, and remediating technical vulnerabilities in OET’s infrastructure and open-source platforms.

## Scope

This policy applies to all OET assets, including:
- Cloud Infrastructure: All resources hosted on Google Cloud Platform.
- Software & Applications: Self-hosted services (e.g., Fleet), GitHub repositories, and internal SaaS tools.
- Endpoints: Laptops and mobile devices used by OET staff and contractors
